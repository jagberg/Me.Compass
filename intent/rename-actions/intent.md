# Intent: Rename actions
Author: Justin Goldberg. Status: draft.

## Problem
Action titles are generated by AI extraction. Sometimes they misread the
ask or are worded awkwardly, and there's no way to fix them. There's also
no way to reword an accurate title into your own shorthand for faster
scanning. The title is fixed once created.

## Proposed outcome
You can edit an action's title inline on the board. Your edit is saved and
treated as yours: a later sync that re-reads the same source must not
overwrite a title you've renamed (the same "my choice wins" behaviour as a
pinned category).

## Affected users and systems
The single user, on the action board. Touches the action list/line UI, the
action update path, and the reconcile/merge step (which must respect a
user-renamed title and leave it alone).

## Constraints
Title only - description, next step and requester stay as extracted for now.
No new data sources or external calls.

## Open questions
Where the edit happens exactly - click the title inline, or an "edit" option
in the ... menu.
Whether a renamed action still shows its original extracted title anywhere
(e.g. kept in history) or is fully replaced.
Whether renaming should also stop the title changing when the action is
merged into or split from another.
